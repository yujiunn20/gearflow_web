GearFlow

Master Gear for Every Complex Activity

Designed for large events and complex logistics. Perfect for film crews, camping trips, or major exhibitions. Stop loading the wrong vehicle and start collaborating.

The Background Story

GearFlow was inspired by a doctor on mobile medical teams. He saw a recurring problem: equipment left behind, items loaded into the wrong vehicles, and logistics plans stored only in a few people's heads. This challenge is universal—whether you're a film crew managing cameras, a group on a massive camping trip, or an event organizer.

We believe logistics should be transparent. With GearFlow's cloud sharing, the loading plan is accessible to everyone. Every team member knows exactly what goes where, ensuring the right items arrive at the right site in the right vehicle.

How to Use

  1. Assign Locations: Set specific gear requirements for each location within an activity.
  2. Designate Vehicles: Map exactly which items go into which transport vehicle to avoid loading chaos.
  3. Real-time Cloud Inventory: Let the whole team sync and check items, reducing dependence on a few key people.

GearFlow

Master Gear for Every Complex Activity

Designed for large events and complex logistics. Perfect for film crews, camping trips, or major exhibitions. Stop loading the wrong vehicle and start collaborating.

Why We Built GearFlow

GearFlow grew out of a real pain point from fieldwork: too much gear, information scattered everywhere, and someone always asking on-site, "Where does this go?", "Is it loaded yet?", "Who's taking this?" It's a common headache for film crews, event productions, camping trips, and field support teams alike.

We wanted to build a tool that brings gear, locations, transport, and workflow together in one place — so teams no longer have to rely on scattered messages, and can instead confirm on-site status quickly from a single shared checklist.

How to Use

  1. Assign Locations: Set specific gear requirements for each location within an activity.
  2. Designate Vehicles: Map exactly which items go into which transport vehicle to avoid loading chaos.
  3. Real-time Cloud Inventory: Let the whole team sync and check items, reducing dependence on a few key people.

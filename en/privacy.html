Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 7, 2026

This privacy policy explains how GearFlow handles and protects your information. GearFlow is a mobile app for managing events, gear, storage, transportation, and checklists.

Information we collect

GearFlow does not require an account and does not actively collect personal identifiers such as your name, email address, phone number, or precise location. Events, warehouses, gear items, transportation records, checklists, and notes that you create in the app are primarily stored locally on your device.

Local data storage

GearFlow uses a local database to save the app data you enter, so you can manage lists and records on the same device. Unless you choose to provide data through device backups, sharing, screenshots, or other external tools, GearFlow does not upload your checklist content to our servers.

Advertising services

GearFlow may use Google Mobile Ads to display advertisements. Google or its partners may process device identifiers, ad interactions, approximate location, or other data related to ad delivery, frequency capping, fraud prevention, and measurement under their own service policies. You can manage personalized advertising preferences through your device settings or Google's ad settings.

Third-party services

Other than advertising services, GearFlow currently does not use login, cloud sync, analytics tracking, or payment services. If new third-party services are added in the future, this policy will be updated.

Data deletion

You can delete individual data in the app, or remove locally stored data by clearing app data or uninstalling the app through your device settings. If your data has been saved by a device backup system, please manage it separately through that backup service.

Children's privacy

GearFlow is not specifically designed for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we may have handled a child's personal information, please contact us.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when features, legal requirements, or services change. The updated policy becomes effective when posted on this page, and the last updated date will be revised.

Contact
